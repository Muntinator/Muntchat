MODEL AND DATASET ATTRIBUTION
=============================

The included CalcGPT 3 checkpoint and ready-to-run model were trained using the
DailyDialog dataset.

DailyDialog citation:

Yanran Li, Hui Su, Xiaoyu Shen, Wenjie Li, Ziqiang Cao, and Shuzi Niu.
"DailyDialog: A Manually Labelled Multi-turn Dialogue Dataset."
Proceedings of IJCNLP 2017.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03957

Dataset distribution referenced by the trainer:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/roskoN/dailydialog
